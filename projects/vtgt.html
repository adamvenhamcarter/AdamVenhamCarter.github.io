Virginia Tech Grand Tour Racing

Program goal

The team prepares and develops a 2001 Mustang GT for endurance competition. My role as team captain includes coordinating technical work, testing, safety preparation, reliability improvements, and race-readiness across the car.

Engineering constraints

Endurance racing rewards more than peak performance. Components, setup changes, and powertrain decisions must balance speed, durability, serviceability, cost, safety, and competition rules.

  • Prioritize on-track time and reliability over fragile peak performance.
  • Develop suspension and alignment solutions within competition constraints.
  • Use dyno and track testing to establish baselines and evaluate changes.
  • Coordinate safety systems, spares, maintenance, inspections, and race logistics.

Technical work

Work has included suspension configuration, engine and intake/exhaust trade studies, ECU/tuning considerations, brake-temperature measurement, dyno testing, engine teardown/rebuild planning, and recurring vehicle inspections.

Leadership

The project also requires translating technical priorities into executable tasks for a multidisciplinary student team. This includes planning work, assigning ownership, coordinating testing, obtaining parts/support, and keeping the program focused on race-critical deliverables.
